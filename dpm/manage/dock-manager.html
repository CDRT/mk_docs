3.5

Dock Manager

Keeps Lenovo dock firmware current and reports dock data to WMI.

Inventory

What it is
A utility you deploy to your ThinkPads that keeps attached Lenovo docks on current firmware and records dock data in WMI.
Requires
Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). The installer, dock_manager_setup.exe, is on Lenovo Support.
Deploy with
Configuration Manager or Intune. Configure it with Group Policy, or with ADMX templates and OMA-URIs in Intune.
Guide
docs.lenovocdrt.com/guides/dm
3.5.1

3.5.1How it works

Dock Manager reduces the effort of deploying dock firmware updates at scale, and it does quite a bit more than firmware. It runs on the PC and keeps a cache of the current firmware for supported Lenovo docks. When a dock with down-level firmware is attached, Dock Manager updates it automatically.

It can download firmware from Lenovo Support over the internet, or from a local repository on your network that you create and maintain with Update Retriever. Search Update Retriever by the dock's four-character machine type to find its latest firmware packages.

3.5.2

3.5.2View and report

  • Dock information and the full update history.
  • Dock data in WMI, under root\Lenovo\Dock_Manager, for remote queries: dock machine type, firmware version, MAC address, and connected devices such as monitors and USB devices.
3.5.3

3.5.3Control and govern

  • Full control of updates through Group Policy.
  • Control which firmware is available through a local repository managed by Update Retriever.
